# Glossary

## Coherence Under Constraint (CUC)
A framework principle stating that persistent structure should be evaluated by how well it maintains coherence under constraints across time.

Related studies: [001](../studies/001-consciousness-models.md), [003](../studies/003-threshold-requirements.md).

## CUC–Ledger
The study sequence and record format used to track claims, thresholds, tests, and falsifiers.

Related archive: [Studies 001–020](../studies/README.md).

## Digital causal body
A bounded, persistent, self-regulating process coupled to an environment through consequential interaction. This is a candidate structure, not a benchmark conclusion.

Related study: [005](../studies/005-digital-causal-body.md).

## The Habitat
A minimal persistent test world containing rooms, objects, signals, energy/stability variables, disturbances, and memory.

Related study: [010](../studies/010-habitat-environment.md).

## BrailleStream
A visual/state encoding layer that folds world-state into a compact symbolic room display and can provide a detached observation layer.

Related study: [010](../studies/010-habitat-environment.md).

## Intrinsic priority generation
An internal action-selection layer that initiates continuity-preserving behavior without immediate external prompting.

Related study: [017](../studies/017-delta-vs-epsilon-intrinsic-priorities.md).

## Recursive Modeling Depth (RMD)
A roadmap axis for measuring how deeply an agent models other agents modeling itself.

Related study: [019](../studies/019-zeta-vs-omega-recursive-modeling.md).

## Field Coherence Level (FCL)
A roadmap axis for measuring whether an agent reasons about isolated self, relation, recursion, or system-level field coherence.

Related study: [020](../studies/020-omega-vs-field-coherence.md).
